# How to use this chart

Due to its unprecedented scale and collaborative nature, books from The Horus Heresy series have been published in an erratic order. Following the publisher’s numbering can spoil and diminish the impact of some events and character developments in later books. To avoid spoiling yourself parts of a title where arrows end, we advise you to read the titles where these arrows come from first.

If a story should be linked to two other stories, but those two other stories are linked together by a chain of stories, then only one of the links will be shown. Example: Titandeath should be linked to The Solar War and Mortis. Except that The Solar War and Mortis are linked together by The Lost and the Damned, then The First Wall and finally Saturnine so we have only displayed the link from Titandeath to The Solar War and not the link from Titandeath to Mortis.

Please note that this flowchart aims to provide a spoiler-free reading order, not a chronological order of the events happening during the Horus Heresy. Certain choices of placements have been made in order to enhance the reading experience. Example: Chronologically Aurelian happens before The First Heretic but if you read Aurelian before, you will spoil yourself some events or twists that happen in The First Heretic.

Titles are displayed depending on their widest distribution media. For instance, some titles have first been published in audio format and then collected in a printed anthology. They are presented as short stories in this diagram. The audios and novellas remaining on the diagram mean that they were never published in anthologies.

This diagram includes all short stories and novels of Horus Heresy Main Series and Siege of Terra currently available. For the Horus Heresy: Primarchs and the Horus Heresy: Characters Series works, some take place entirely before or after the Horus Heresy. That’s why we decided to include only the ones that really take place during Horus Heresy and help the overall understanding. They are labelled “Primarchs” or “Characters Series” below the title.

In order to make this flowchart, we had to make choices and some of these choices will certainly be open to debate. We have done the best we can to come as close as possible to the ideal graphic we had envisioned and to try to make this graphic and this great series more accessible.

Our team was unable to establish any other relevant links. If you find any errors or new connections, please contact us on our social media or the Black Librarium message board.
